# AGENTS.md

- Vue SFCs: `<script setup lang="ts">`, then `<template>`, then `<style>`.
- UI is Tailwind 4 in the "Pop" look: warm paper (`paper`), white cards, violet brand (`violet`), bright stickers (`lime`, `pink`, `orange`, `sky`, `butter`) with soft depth — no outlines, no hard offset shadows. Inter Tight for everything (`.hd`: italic 900, tight, often lowercase, for headings and big numbers); Caveat (`font-hand` / `.hand`) only for compliments and handwritten notes. Use the theme tokens and component classes in `src/style.css` (`hd`, `lbl`, `pill`, `chip`, `btn` + `btn-primary` / `btn-dark` / `btn-pink`, `card`, `sticker`, `tilt`, `tag`, `avatar`) before custom CSS. Reka UI for dialogs, comboboxes and popovers (`AppDialog`, `AppCombobox`); Lucide for icons.
- Ribbons are smooth rising sine bands in each challenge's group colours: always generate them with `src/lib/waves.ts` (via `Ribbons.vue`), never draw squiggles by hand. The logo is `StepsMark.vue` (soft white steps on a violet tile; source of truth `assets/Challenge.icon`).
- Motion makes it feel friendly (sheets rise, lists and stickers pop in, numbers count up, confetti on wins): use the `anim-*` classes, `<Transition>` names (`fade`, `pop`, `rise`, `list`) and `src/lib/motion.ts` (`burst`, `ripple`, `animate`, `stagger`). Always honour `prefers-reduced-motion`: CSS motion is cut globally in `src/style.css`; JS motion checks `prefersReducedMotion`.
- Dancers are aged about 4 to teens: every word is cheering and wholesome. No sarcasm, nothing that could sting, no telling them off.
- Feel like a native app: back and forward restore scroll (`src/router/index.ts`), tabs replace history and scroll to top when tapped again, sheets drag down to close (`AppDialog`), no double-tap zoom or long-press callouts, inputs at least 16px so iOS doesn't zoom on focus.
- Use native inputs (`inputmode="numeric"` for amounts), never on-screen keypads; give each form field its own full-width row, as names run long.
- The compliment heart is an easter egg: small, quiet, light-grey outline, no label. Tapping it plays its animation (squish and fill pink, pop with little hearts, ripple), then the compliment sheet rises.
- Firestore data is shared with the live app: keep collection and field names (`challenges`, `groups`, `participants`, `entries`, `complimentsSubmitted`, `compliments`, `flags`). Writes go through `src/stores/app.ts`.
- Lists of groups, participants and entries must filter on `challengeId` (or, for entries, on your own `createdBy`): the rules can only check those (that's what keeps private challenges private). Challenges list with `private == false`; private ones are read by id.
- `$`-prefixed stats (`$total`, `$count`, and on challenges `$dancers`, `$lastEntryAt`) are written only by the `aggregateStatistics` Cloud Function; never write them from the client. `syncAggregateStatistics` rebuilds them all.
- Explore lists public challenges while they run: dated ones until 2 weeks after they end, open-ended ones until 60 days without an entry (`src/lib/date.ts`). New challenges default to link-only; listed ones need an end date.
- Admins (`admin` custom claim, from `ADMIN_EMAILS` at sign-in) and a challenge's owner can fix any item in it. Flags are for admins only: emailed to `ADMIN_EMAILS`, never shown to owners or other users.
- Entries are owned by their `createdBy` uid (see `firestore.rules`): anonymous, or `sd_<ScotDance.app uid>` after "Sign in with ScotDance.app", which moves that device's anonymous docs to the account (`functions/src/index.ts`).
- ScotDance.app buttons ("Sign in with ScotDance.app", "Send to ScotDance.app") look the same on every tool, whatever the tool's own colours: blue `#1a6fd0` fill, white bold label, Scott at 16px before it (`src/components/ScotDanceMark.vue`, from ScotDance's `web/public/img/favicon.svg`). The reference is Splits' Send button (`scotdance-splits`: `src/components/ExportSettingsDialog.vue`).
- Account email and name live on the Auth user, never in Firestore (every document is publicly readable).
- Run lint, type-check and `npm run test:e2e` (Node 22) before committing.
